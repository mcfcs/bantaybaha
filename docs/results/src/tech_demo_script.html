Bantay-Baha: technical demo script (2:50)

PJDSC 2026 / technical demo

About 300 words of narration at a calm pace (~140 words a minute), leaving time for the clicks and redraws. This is a feature-by-feature tour of the dashboard: every control, tab and column, and what it tells a health officer. Anonymous: no names, team name, school or logos on screen or in the voice-over. Record the dashboard full screen at 1920 x 1080, browser zoom 90%, with Typhoon Ompong selected and the track at "no shift" before you start. Cues in bold are what you click.

timeon screennarration
0:00-0:15Dashboard, Action map tab. Slowly point down the sidebar: Typhoon, the two track sliders, How to read this."This is the Bantay-Baha dashboard. On the left you choose a typhoon and, if you want, move its track. Everything else updates from that choice."

Every column header explains itself on hover. For Ompong, 22 provinces should pre-position supplies."

1:35-2:00Provinces tab. Scroll right along Pampanga's row: R 0.83 and R raw 0.50, A range 4.8-6.4%, cases 934 (337-1,162), under-5 and 60+ cut off, Sentinel, Main drivers. Click Download table (CSV)."Provinces is the full ranked table. Pampanga's surge risk is 83 percent after calibration. Its access loss is shown as a range, because two methods measure it: a road-and-ferry network and a travel-time map. You get expected cases with a 90 percent range, children and older people cut off, and the main drivers of the risk. One click downloads it."
2:00-2:15Municipalities tab: choose Pampanga. Point at Candaba (36%), Apalit, San Luis."Municipalities drills down to towns. In Pampanga, Candaba loses the most access, so that is where a mobile clinic should go."
2:15-2:35Outbreak graph (sentinels), then Forecast skill: the model comparison, then Weeks ahead 4 for the fan chart. Then Validation."Outbreak graph shows which provinces rise first. Forecast skill compares our model with the baseline and three benchmarks, with every forecast's range. Validation shows whether high-priority provinces really surged, and how stable the ranking is."
2:35-2:50Leptospirosis (lag chart), Data & caveats, then Usability test. End on the Action map."Leptospirosis shows how cases follow rain. Data and caveats lists every limit next to the numbers. And a built-in usability test lets health officers rate the tool."
